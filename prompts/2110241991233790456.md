## Original post

I feel like we haven't seen enough of what Gemini 4 Argon can actually do ❤️‍🔥

I gave it one image of a Gothic Victorian carriage and asked it to build the whole thing as an interactive 3D experience.

Just one image and this is what you get. 

A 174,000 face model from scratch, complete with opening doors, hinged windows, working wheel physics, suspension, flickering lanterns and even custom sculpted skulls.

All of this generated through code. No blender. 

Argon absolutely delivered with this one too. Many more such experiments are on the way :D
