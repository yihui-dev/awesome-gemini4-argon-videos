## Original post

I’m still half in disbelief 
We asked Gemini Argon to teach the Apollo humanoid to play cornhole, and it zero-shotted it

All we gave it was a video of a person doing it. It then took control of the robot, generated its own sim env, performed some sysID, and trained launches on the real robot. 

It took it a few real shots to improve and then land many consecutive airmails. 

The process was so long and articulated that we asked the robot itself to explain what it did to us, as you can see at the end of the video. It even came up with the gestures

Great demo for a great model @googledeepmind @apptronik
