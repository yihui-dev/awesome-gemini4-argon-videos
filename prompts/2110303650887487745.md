## Original post

Asked Argon to make me an interactive musical garden and it rendered me this!

It made an infinite 3D digital garden and generative concert-harp instrument that responds to real-time webcam hand gestures and your cursor. I especially loved the way the river was rendered in the night time.

Argon was able to add the following : 

1. 3D Rendering & Custom Shaders: Three.js (WebGL2) with custom GLSL Vertex & Fragment Shaders, GPU-instanced botanical geometry (82,000 multi-blade grass tufts, 22,000 3D sculpted wildflower bouquets, 4,200 bokeh fireflies, and 1,800 aerodynamic 3D petals), toroidal infinite world wrapping, and procedural Simplex/FBM terrain & water shaders

2. MediaPipe Tasks Vision (HandLandmarker) with an adaptive 1-Euro Low-Pass Filter for low-latency 3D webcam hand tracking

3. Procedural Audio Synthesis: Web Audio API custom multi-partial acoustic concert-harp synthesizer (D Major 9 / G Lydian harmonic chord voicings, stereo hall convolution reverb, shimmer delay, and pink-noise breeze/brook generators) requirin g zero external audio files.

Made in @antigravity with @GeminiApp 4 Argon. And final touches in @GoogleAIStudio.

Link : http://enchanted-meadow.ai.studio
